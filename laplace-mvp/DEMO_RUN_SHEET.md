# FORESIGHT demo run sheet

## Opening
“FORESIGHT predicts what a physical structure will do before you change it, asks for what it can’t see, and then lets reality check its answer.”

## Demonstration
1. Show the live phone camera and say the tower model is operator-confirmed. The glasses are not integrated in this prototype.
2. Select an uncertain support in the tower model. Assess a proposed removal and show the calculated alternatives. Identify the uncertainty as a deliberately withheld fact if you already know it. The automatic clarification is application-generated.
3. Restore the known state using the operator's confirmation. Record a question with Muse Voice Transcribe and show a live Muse Spark answer. The spoken response uses browser speech synthesis.
4. Assess a present block. Show the static prediction, then play the separate experimental physics replay; call out disagreements or substantial movement. Lock the prediction before touching the tower.
5. Perform the move, capture it on a continuous external recording, and record the true result. Mark assisted if another hand supports the tower. Never imply a previously reported trial was recorded if it was not.
6. Show the evidence trace, including the locked prediction, operator outcome and whether comparison is valid.

## What the system can and cannot claim
- SAM 3.1 returns candidate boxes from a captured image; it does not automatically build the block model.
- The isometric model is user-supplied, not a 360-degree reconstruction.
- The static calculation is an idealized hypothesis; the physics replay is experimental and can be unstable. Neither is a real-world safety assessment.
- A prediction hash helps identify a saved record; it is not independent proof of timing.
- Two prior unassisted outcomes were user-reported matches, without continuous video. Assisted trials are not comparable.

## Recording
Use a screen recorder that captures the FORESIGHT window and your microphone. Make a short test first. Keep the phone's live camera visible in the app and verify that the recording includes the tower, the locked result, the physical action and the result. The phone's live stream is not itself a recording. Preserve the original clip.
